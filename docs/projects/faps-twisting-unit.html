Wire Twisting Unit

FAU – Institute FAPS | Design and build

Motor-driven wire twisting module

AutomationDesignWire harnessStepper driveArduino3D printing

What?

  • A compact twisting unit for the Robotik Challenge setup: it holds the wire end in a connector holder and turns it to twist the wire.

How?

  • Designed the bracket, motor coupling and connector holder around an igus stepper motor and a rotary feedthrough.
  • Closed-loop stepper drive (CL42T) with an Arduino Nano; speed tests up to 600 rpm.
  • Printed parts for fast iterations; mounts directly on the task board.

Results

  • Built and tested unit, integrated in the Robotik Challenge task board.
  • Makes wire twisting – a typical wire-harness process step – available as a robot task.
