PURA

Arche Robotics | 2020 – 2023 | Mechanical design engineer

Cleans the air of 300 people per hour

RoboticsDesignPhotocatalysisAir sensing

What?

  • PURA is based on the AT1 platform and sanitizes surfaces and floors and purifies the air.
  • Designed a sanitation chamber with a photocatalyst module and HOCl disinfection liquid.
  • Senses air quality: particles, VOC, humidity, temperature.

How?

  • Photocatalyst module with 16 ceramic foams coated with TiO2 and 40 UVA LEDs.
  • A piezo fog generator makes a dry fog of disinfectant, blown by a DC fan at 150 m3/h.
  • No consumables such as HEPA filters, cartridges or mercury UVC bulbs.

Results

  • Deployed an autonomous disinfection robot that works 24/7.
  • Cleans as much air per hour as 300 people breathe.
  • Works without harmful chemicals.
  • Built strategic relationships with Chinese manufacturers.
