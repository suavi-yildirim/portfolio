AT1 Mobile Platform

Arche Robotics | 2020 – 2023 | Mechanical design engineer

AMR platform for 50 kg payload

RoboticsDesignSolidWorksFusion 360FEA

What?

  • AT1 is an indoor autonomous mobile robot platform (AMR).
  • A versatile platform for multiple applications.
  • Designed the chassis for a 50 kg payload.

How?

  • Used SolidWorks and Fusion 360 for the mechanical design.
  • Ran an FEA study of the forces and moments on the chassis.
  • 3 mm 6061 aluminium sheet-metal parts joined by TIG welding.

Results

  • Launched AT1, now used from healthcare to retail and hospitality.
  • Basis for ongoing development of new hardware and software modules.
