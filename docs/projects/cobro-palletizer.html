Cobot Palletizer

Cobro Robot Technologies | 2023 – 2024 | Concept design engineer

Won follow-up projects

RoboticsAutomationABB CRB 15000CobotStructural analysis

What?

  • Developed a mobile palletizing robot with collaborative robots that moves easily between production lines for more flexibility and efficiency.

How?

  • Performed static and dynamic analyses to design a robust base fixture.
  • Selected the ABB CRB 15000 cobot with a 12 kg payload.

Results

  • The system was successful and helped the company win further projects, as it stands out from stationary palletizers on the market.
