Immersive Design Challenge

SONY

Siemens × Sony | 12/2024 – 04/2025 | Team lead

1st place of 900 participants

DesignAI / SimulationXRDigital TwinNX

What?

  • The Immersive Design Challenge is a global engineering competition organized by Siemens Digital Industries Software in collaboration with Sony.
  • Encourages sustainable design by bringing immersive technologies such as XR and digital twins into real engineering problems.
  • Scale: ~900 students, 230+ universities, 38 countries.

How?

  • Led the team NextCycle.
  • Developed BatteryTwin-XR: a Siemens-integrated digital twin for a sustainable EV battery lifecycle.
  • Designed an XR framework with the Sony XR head-mounted display to support battery monitoring, reuse and recycling decisions.
  • Focused on circular economy: better recycling efficiency, less battery waste.

Results

  • Won 1st place among the international finalist teams.
  • Recognized by Siemens and Sony for an innovative, sustainability-driven XR solution.
  • Awarded a Sony XR headset and a 1-year NX Immersive Designer license for academic and research use.
  • Invited to Siemens Realize LIVE; helped expand immersive design capabilities at FAU.
