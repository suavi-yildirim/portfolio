Torsional Measurement Device

2E Design | 2023 – 2024 | Mechanical design

Anti-roll bar stiffness tuning

DesignTest rigLoad cellMotorsport

What?

  • Developed a torsional measurement device for analysing the mechanical characteristics of anti-roll bars in automobiles.

How?

  • Implemented a hub and bearing setup to assess anti-roll bar twisting.
  • Transferred the load through lever arms to a 1-ton capacity load cell for accurate measurement.

Results

  • The device allowed measurement and adjustment of anti-roll bar twist stiffness, ensuring optimal performance for the targeted racing vehicles.
