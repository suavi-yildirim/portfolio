Robotics & AI Hackathon – Teach the Robot

Siemens Healthineers | 10/2026 | Robotics & Simulation Engineer, team of 6

12× faster demo generation

RoboticsAI / SimulationIsaac SimGenesisLeRobotVLAQuest 3

What?

  • 3-day Siemens Healthineers Robotics & AI Hackathon 2026 in Erlangen: build the learning loop for a vision-language-action (VLA) robot companion in the cathlab.
  • Task: bimanual box handover with a Franka FR3 Duo in simulation.

How?

  • Robotics & Simulation Engineer in a 6-person team.
  • Built a scripted IK expert in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6.0 that generates demos with moved box poses (16/18 successful).
  • Fine-tuned π0.5 (LoRA) and ACT; rebuilt the task in Genesis for parallel data generation.

Results

  • Showed that the data, not the model, was the bottleneck: the 94 provided demos contain one single motion.
  • 12× faster demo generation: from 6 min to about 30 s per demo.
  • Prototyped VR teleoperation with Meta Quest 3 for human demos.
