Promobot

Arche Robotics | 2020 – 2023 | Mechanical design engineer

Deployed at MediaMarkt, 400 customers/month

RoboticsAMRRetailDashboards

What?

  • Promobot helps businesses engage customers and build their brand in physical and digital stores.
  • Features: greeting, guidance, marketing, AR.

How?

  • A data-driven companion that supports digital transformation.
  • Monthly dashboards of sensor data such as air quality and demographics.

Results

  • Deployed at MediaMarkt Metropol Istanbul on 2 Jan 2023.
  • Interacted with 400 customers per month on average.
  • Drove 10+ km autonomously per month without mechanical issues.
