Generative Design Study

Arche Robotics | 2020 – 2023 | Mechanical design engineer

30% lighter part

DesignAI / SimulationFusion 360Generative design

What?

  • Used generative design (AI-driven algorithms) to create lightweight parts that meet performance and design requirements.

How?

  • Used the Fusion 360 generative design toolbox.
  • Defined the loads on the part; compared von Mises stress results to ensure structural integrity.

Results

  • 30% less weight with all design constraints and performance criteria met.
